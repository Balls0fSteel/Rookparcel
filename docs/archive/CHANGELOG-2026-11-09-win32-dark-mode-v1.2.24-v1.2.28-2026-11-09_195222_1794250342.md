## 2026-11-09_171847_1794241127: Version 1.2.28: Win32 dark mode: right-aligned list cells kept their text; the live switch checked

- **Found by the live-switch test:** under `DarkMode_Explorer` the list view pads its cells a little more than the plain list, so a right-aligned number at the edge of its column ("7.71 GB" in the 70 px Size column) came out as "7.71 ..." in dark, and stayed so after a switch back to light (the class is reset, the padding is not). The column widths, DPI, fonts and rectangles were measured identical (`tools/win32-shots/measure-columns.ps1`). Fix: in dark, `controls::dark_cell_text` draws the text of right-aligned cells itself with the plain list's padding and skips the default drawing (left-aligned cells sit where they sit in light); wired into the three list custom-draw paths. The `DarkMode_ItemsView` class was tried for the list body and pads the same.
- **Live switch (`tools/win32-shots/switch-test.ps1`, hidden desktop):** Dark → Light → Dark through Preferences > UI Settings > Theme with posted messages: the main window, a dialog and the Preferences page follow at once; the light picture after a switch equals the light baseline but for the caption's version and timing texts (the Size column before this fix); the dark picture after the switch back equals a fresh dark start; the ini ends with the chosen value.
- **Noted, not changed (BACKLOG "Win32"):** the queue column shows nothing for "*" rows in light (the plain list hides the one-character text in its 28 px column, most likely the subitem image room `LVS_EX_SUBITEMIMAGES` reserves); our dark drawing shows the "*". The same in 1.2.23, so light stays as it was.
- **Validation:** Win32 122 tests, clippy and fmt clean; light capture equal to the baseline (caption's version, Theme row and timing apart); dark capture reviewed: "7.71 GB", "0 (163)" and the other right-aligned cells complete.

## 2026-11-09_165550_1794239750: Version 1.2.27: Win32 dark mode, slices 5 to 7: every dialog, Preferences, the scheduler grid, a switch while dialogs are open

- **One line per dialog procedure:** `theme::dialog_message(dlg, msg, wp, lp)` at the top of the 15 procedures (Add, Create, Properties, RSS feed and Downloader, Search Results/Sources/Source, the helper's site picker, Connect to Remote, Setup Guide, Notices, the countdown, the updater, the closing window, the text prompt, Preferences): `WM_INITDIALOG` runs `theme::dialog_init` (the caption, then every child by class: push buttons `DarkMode_Explorer`, check boxes and radios the glyph plus a painted caption, group boxes a painted frame and caption, etched lines a painted rule, edits and combo boxes `DarkMode_CFD` with the drop list `DarkMode_Explorer`, list views, tree views and tab controls as the main window's, progress bars our colours with the visual style off, tooltips, up-downs and scroll bars `DarkMode_Explorer`); the `WM_CTLCOLOR*` family is answered in dark; `WM_APP_THEME` re-themes. Nothing of it changes light: the subclasses do nothing there and no theme or colour is set until dark is in force.
- **Specifics:** the Add dialog's check images are drawn over the list's surface colour; the Setup Guide keeps its grey help texts through the theme; every tooltip (`help::Tips`, `control_tip`, the Add dialog's) takes the dark class; the scheduler grid (`sched_grid.rs`) fills, texts, grid lines and disabled greys through the theme (the Off cell the surface colour, the lines the border colour in dark); the marquee progress bar and `MessageBoxW` stay the system's.
- **Slice 7:** a switch (the preference, or Windows' setting under System) reaches every open dialog too: `theme::broadcast` posts `WM_APP_THEME` to the thread's `#32770` windows and each re-runs `dialog_init`; the main window's `apply_theme` rebuilds the icon sets through `apply_dpi`. The dead-code allowance of `theme.rs` is gone with the last slice. `tools/win32-shots/capture.ps1` (from the session's scratch) captures the main window, every dialog and every Preferences page on the hidden desktop for the pixel comparison.
- **Docs:** D64 in DECISIONS.md, DARKMODE.md marked built, BACKLOG "Themes" lists the hand checks left (a 1809 VM, 150/200 % DPI, a live flip of Windows' setting with dialogs open), the wiki gains a Dark mode page and a Theme (Windows) row in the Preferences reference (the wiki repository still needs the copy).
- **Validation:** Win32 122 tests (a hidden dialog with one control of each class is themed without a panic, and light leaves the `WM_CTLCOLOR*` family alone); clippy clean for Win32, frontend and (type-checked for Linux) GTK; the light capture equals the baseline (caption's version, the Theme row and timing apart: the baseline's Properties picture had been taken before its tab and buttons painted); dark captures of all ten dialogs and the twelve Preferences pages reviewed: dark surfaces, captions, frames, lists, combos and the Theme drop-down reading "Dark". Found and fixed in review: group box captions showed their `&` literally (now the dialog's keyboard-cue state decides, as for the themed caption).

The tab strip is painted by `theme::tab_control`'s subclass in dark (strip, tabs with their icons and texts, the body's frame joined to the selected tab); the four detail list pages and the Pieces list go through `theme::list`.
- **Validation:** Win32 121 tests; the light capture equals the baseline but for the caption's version, the Theme row and timing (Logger, RSS, a caret); in dark the main window is now dark throughout. Dialogs stay light until the next slice.

## 2026-11-09_164250_1794238970: Version 1.2.25: Win32 dark mode, slices 2 and 3: the main window's controls, toolbar, menu bar and icons

- **Lists, tree, status bar, Files controls (slice 2):** `controls::row_background` and `selection_colours` read `theme::sys()` (light: the same `GetSysColor` bytes; dark: the row shading lightens instead of darkening); `theme::list` gives a list view and its header the `DarkMode_Explorer`/`DarkMode_ItemsView` classes, the dark background and text colours and a header painted by a subclass (`DarkMode_ItemsView` draws black text); `theme::tree` the tree view; `theme::status_bar` paints the status bar's parts, icons, dividers and size grip in dark; `theme::check_box` paints a check box's caption (the themed button ignores `WM_CTLCOLORSTATIC`) over the theme's dark glyph; `theme::ctl_color` answers the `WM_CTLCOLOR*` family; the main window fills its client area and the splitter gaps in dark (`WM_ERASEBKGND`) and the Files tab's count and Tree view box take the window colours.
- **Toolbar, menu bar, icons (slice 3):** the toolbar's `NM_CUSTOMDRAW` paints hot and pressed buttons and the search drop-down's chevron in dark (`TBCDRF_NOBACKGROUND | NOEDGES | USECDCOLORS`); the menu bar answers `WM_UAHDRAWMENU`/`WM_UAHDRAWMENUITEM` and paints the line under it after `WM_NCPAINT`/`WM_NCACTIVATE` (popups come from `SetPreferredAppMode`); every icon builder (`toolbar_images`, `state_icon`, `ui_images`, the menu bitmaps, whose cache key gains the palette) picks the dark PNG set (`frontend::icons::toolbar_bgra` gains `dark`); a theme switch rebuilds them through `apply_dpi`; the tooltips of the toolbar, list and tree take the dark class; the navigation image list is freed at exit (it leaked).
- **Still light in dark after this:** the detail tab strip, the General/Pieces/Speed panels, the detail lists and every dialog (slices 4-6).
- **Validation:** Win32 121 tests; clippy and fmt clean. Hidden-desktop captures (`shots/capture-baseline.ps1`, 33 PNGs each): the light run of this build equals the 1.2.23 baseline pixel for pixel except the version in the caption, the UI Settings page (the new Theme row), and timing differences (the Logger's lines, the RSS dialog's feed list, a blinking caret, a help pane or General panel caught mid-paint, confirmed by re-capturing); the dark run shows the list, header, tree, toolbar, menu bar, status bar, Files controls and icons dark.

## 2026-11-09_162552_1794237952: Version 1.2.24: Win32 dark mode, slice 1 of 8: the Theme preference and the dark caption

- **Owner request (2026-11-09): the Windows dark mode "we need it"**, all eight slices of [DARKMODE.md](docs/DARKMODE.md) on this branch and one release at the end; light must stay pixel-identical meanwhile; a Windows that cannot show dark (before 10 1809, or `uxtheme.dll` without the exports) stays light and says so under the Theme drop-down; System (Windows' *Choose your mode* for apps) is the default, Light and Dark are forced.
- **Preference `ui.theme`** (`system|light|dark`, the Web UI's ids) in `GuiOptions`, on `pref_pages`' UI Settings page behind `Features.theme` (true only for Win32; GTK and macOS follow their desktops) with help text `PrefThemeDesktop`; the Win32 dialog's Display group gets the Theme drop-down (`ID_THEME`, the `ID_LANG` pattern) and a note line (`PrefThemeUnavailable`, in all 35 tables); the groups below move down 28 DLU.
- **`crates/win32/src/theme.rs`:** `Mode`, the pure `resolve` rule (High Contrast and old Windows always light; System follows Windows, unknown counts as light), `uxtheme.dll`'s exports by ordinal (132/133/135/136/104, feature-tested once, `AllowDarkModeForApp` on 17763, `SetPreferredAppMode` from 18362), Windows' setting from `ShouldAppsUseDarkMode` or the `AppsUseLightTheme` DWORD (`integration::get_dword_in`), `SPI_GETHIGHCONTRAST`, the dark `SysPalette` (aligned with `palette::DARK` and the Web UI's dark tokens, text ≥ 4.5:1 over every surface by test), brushes, `theme_window` (DWM caption, attribute 19 before 18985) and `theme_control` for the later slices. `init` runs before any window; `WM_SETTINGCHANGE`/`WM_THEMECHANGED`/`WM_SYSCOLORCHANGE` and a changed preference re-resolve and post `WM_APP_THEME`, which re-themes the caption. Test hooks `ROOKPARCEL_FORCE_WINDOWS_DARK`, `ROOKPARCEL_FORCE_HIGH_CONTRAST`, `ROOKPARCEL_NO_DARK_UXTHEME`.
- **Fixed in passing:** "Alternate list background color" only took effect at the next start (`apply_options_from` repainted without `set_alternate_rows`).
- **What is dark after this slice:** the caption of the main window and the text prompt, and the popup menus; everything else is still light on purpose. Features `Win32_Graphics_Dwm` and `Win32_UI_Accessibility` added.
- **Validation:** frontend 364 tests, Win32 121 (7 new in `theme::tests`), clippy and fmt clean. The light baseline screenshots of 1.2.23 were captured on the hidden desktop before this slice for the pixel comparison at the end.
