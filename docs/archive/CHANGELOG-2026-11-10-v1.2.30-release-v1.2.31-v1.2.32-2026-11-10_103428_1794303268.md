## 2026-11-10_083446_1794296086: Version 1.2.32: the dark status bar's help text; the torrent list's cells without icon room

- **Both seen in the owner's screenshots of 1.2.30 and reproduced on the hidden desktop.**
- **Dark status bar empty while a menu item or toolbar button is highlighted:** `paint_status` asked the control for its simple-mode text (`SB_GETTEXTW` with `SB_SIMPLEID`), which answers nothing, so the help line was never drawn in dark and the bar looked empty (`menu-file-main.png` of `tools/win32-shots`). **Fix:** `status_proc` keeps the text of each `SB_SETTEXTW` for `SB_SIMPLEID` (per status bar, dropped at `WM_NCDESTROY`) and `paint_status` draws it.
- **Size cut to "1.51 ..." and `#` empty in light after a switch from dark (`switch-test.ps1`):** with `LVS_EX_SUBITEMIMAGES` the list keeps an icon's room in every cell that answers `I_IMAGENONE`. The plain list applies it to left-aligned cells only; once a visual-style class has been set (dark, or light after a switch, `SetWindowTheme(NULL)` does not undo it) the right-aligned cells lose it too. This is the padding `controls::dark_cell_text` was written around in 1.2.2x, and the cause of the `#` column's missing `*` (BACKLOG, found 2026-11-09). **Fix:** `LVN_GETDISPINFOW` answers -1 outside the Name column, which leaves no room. **Visible change in both themes:** left-aligned cells of the torrent list (Status, Label and the like) start 18 px further left, at the header text's position; the Name column is unchanged.
- **Validation:** captures of dark, light, the File menu open in both, and dark > light > dark by the Theme combo: the help line shows in dark, Size and `#` are whole after the switch. Light differs from 1.2.31 only in the caption's version and those cells. Clippy `-D warnings`, fmt, the 122 Win32 tests on one thread. **Not checked:** the user's real session by hand; `dark_cell_text` is kept (after a switch right-aligned numbers sit 2 px left of a fresh light start).

## 2026-11-10_082302_1794295382: Version 1.2.31: Win32 dark lists without the column lines below the rows

- **Reported by the owner (screenshots of 1.2.30):** in dark every report list showed a thin line down each column edge, from the last row to the bottom of the list; light has none. Not ours: `DarkMode_Explorer`'s list view draws them (2 px, `#404043` and `#1c2632`), and the rows cover them, so only the empty area showed them. Reproduced on the hidden desktop with `tools/win32-shots` (`-DemoCount 4`).
- **Tried first, dropped:** `DarkMode_ItemsView` on the list view keeps the lines and turns the scroll bars light.
- **Fix (`theme::paint_list`, called from `list_proc`):** in dark, a report list with room below its last row paints into a bitmap (`WM_PAINT` with the device context in `wParam`), each line is replaced by the pixel column beside it (not a plain fill, so a selection rectangle dragged over the empty area stays whole), and the bitmap goes to the screen with the header's rectangle excluded (the list does not clip its header; a first try blanked it). Lists with rows down to the bottom, other views and light take the control's own paint as before.
- **Validation:** dark captures of the main window with 4 and 60 demo torrents, all detail tabs and the dialogs: no line left (pixel check at a column edge), header dividers, scroll bars, selection and progress bars as before; light captures before and after identical except the carets and the Logger's lines. Clippy `-D warnings` and fmt clean for the Win32 crate; its tests green on one thread. **Not checked:** a selection rectangle and a column resize by hand (no pointer input on the hidden desktop), and 150/200 % (the covered strip is 4 px left and 1 px right of the edge, scaled).
- **Seen in the same screenshots:** the dark window's status bar was empty, and the light list cut Size to "1.51 ..."; both fixed in 1.2.32, above.
