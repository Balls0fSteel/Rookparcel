How to open Rookparcel

macOS blocks Rookparcel the first time, because the app is not notarised by Apple. Allowing it takes a minute and is needed only once.

1. Copy it to Applications

Drag Rookparcel.app onto the Applications folder in this window. Then open it from Applications, not from this disk image.

2. Allow it: macOS 15 Sequoia and newer

  1. Double-click Rookparcel in Applications. macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
    “Rookparcel” Not Opened Apple could not verify “Rookparcel” is free of malware…
    Move to TrashDone
  2. Open System Settings (Apple menu) and click Privacy & Security in the sidebar.
  3. Scroll down to Security. Next to “Rookparcel was blocked…”, click Open Anyway. The button is there for about an hour after step 1.
    Security “Rookparcel” was blocked to protect your Mac.
    Open Anyway
  4. Confirm with Open Anyway, then your password or Touch ID.

From then on Rookparcel opens with a double-click like any other app.

2. Allow it: macOS 11 to 14

  1. In Applications, Control-click (or right-click) Rookparcel and choose Open.
  2. In the warning, click Open.

If it says “is damaged”, or you prefer the Terminal

Open Terminal (in Applications > Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Rookparcel.app

It removes the “downloaded from the internet” mark from the copy in Applications; nothing else changes. Then open Rookparcel as usual.

Why macOS asks

Apple verifies only apps whose makers pay for its developer programme and send each version to Apple (“notarisation”). Rookparcel does not do that yet, so macOS cannot tell who made it and warns. To check that your download is the published file, compare its checksum with SHA256SUMS of the release.

The first time, macOS may also ask whether Rookparcel may find devices on your local network. Allow it if you want local peer discovery and port mapping on your router.
